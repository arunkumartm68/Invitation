## ✨ Sections Included

Guests first choose **ಕನ್ನಡ** or **English** before opening the envelope. English keeps the existing invitation wording and design. Kannada includes locally adapted wedding wording, names, ceremony details, venue information, calendar entries, and copied addresses. The floating **ಕನ್ನಡ / English** button lets guests change languages at any time. Kannada translations are maintained in `languages.js`.

1. **Envelope Opening Ritual:**
   - 3D interactive royal envelope with authentic wax seal and golden `G & P` crest.
   - Tap to unseal the invitation with smooth animations and falling rose petals & golden sparklers.
2. **Hero Presentation:**
   - Invocational blessing (*OM SHRI GANESHAYA NAMAHA*).
   - Couple's names in gold foil calligraphy.
   - Dual-ceremony cards for **Reception (14th Nov)** and **Auspicious Muhurtha (15th Nov)**.
   - Live synchronized countdown clock.
   - "Add to Calendar" and "View on Google Maps" buttons.
3. **The Auspicious Union:**
   - Groom & Bride profile cards with parents' blessings and wedding hashtag.
4. **Celebration Itinerary & Events:**
   - **Grand Wedding Reception** (Saturday, 14th Nov, 7:30 PM)
   - **Sacred Muhurtha** (Sunday, 15th Nov, 9:15 AM - 10:45 AM)
5. **The Wedding Destination (Venue):**
   - Full venue address: *Hebbalu, NH-4 Highway, near hebbalu Toll plaza ,Davanagere District, Karnataka - 577556*.
